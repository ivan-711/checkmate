## Things a fresh builder must know
- No entrance on load, Reset, Undo or tab return: `Organism` morphs only on review/confirm → linked (`morphMode`), crossfade under reduced motion, everything else `initial={false}`.
- The fragments' exit lasts 0.95 s (fill keyframes) so they stay under the record until it is opaque; AnimatePresence `custom={{sheet, mode}}` carries the mode to the exiting children.
- Motion animates full `transform` strings and `clipPath` (compositor); the mulberry fill is an opacity overlay. No CSS transitions on those properties in `organism.css`.
- `Fragment` keeps the constructed chip outside the clipped body so its tooltip is not cut.
- Nothing hard-codes hero names, dates or drugs; labels map only enums (`ER_FHIR` → "Emergency room").
